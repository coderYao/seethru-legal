Terms of Service

Effective November 2, 2026

What this is

SeeThru Uploader is a small command-line tool. The owner of the SeeThru channel runs it on their own computer to post SeeThru's own videos to the SeeThru YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the SeeThru channel uses it. It is not offered to the public, and there are no user sign-ups.

Platform rules

Videos posted with this tool stay subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and TikTok's Terms of Service. The tool uses YouTube API Services.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, with no guarantee that it will work without errors or stay available.
