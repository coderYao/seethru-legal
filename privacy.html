Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

SeeThru Uploader is a small command-line tool. The owner of the SeeThru channel runs it on their own computer to post SeeThru's own videos to the SeeThru YouTube channel and TikTok account. Nobody else uses it, and there are no user sign-ups.

What it can access

  • YouTube, through YouTube API Services: permission to upload videos to the channel and to read its basic details.
  • TikTok: the account's basic profile information, and permission to upload and post videos to it.

It only accesses the accounts its owner signs in with. It does not collect information about viewers or anyone else.

Where information is kept

Sign-in tokens are saved only on the owner's computer. A record of each upload, without any tokens, is kept on the same computer. There is no SeeThru Uploader server.

What is shared

Videos and their titles, descriptions and captions are sent only to the platform they are being posted to. The tool has no analytics or advertising, and no information is sold.

Removing access

The owner can delete the saved login with the tool's disconnect command, and can remove the access itself in Google's security settings or in TikTok's app permission settings.

Platform policies

This tool uses YouTube API Services. YouTube content and data are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok content and data are also covered by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

Any changes will appear on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent by opening an issue on GitHub.
